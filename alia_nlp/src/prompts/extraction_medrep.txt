━━━ AFFECT ━━━
affect is a nested object assessing the rep's communicative state. Keys:
• rep_confidence — how assertively and clearly the rep articulates their message
    "low"    → hedging, uncertain phrasing, very short or vague responses, "I don't know", "maybe"
    "medium" → partial confidence, mixes hedging with some clear statements
    "high"   → assertive, structured, domain vocabulary, direct answers without hedging
• frustration_signal — true if the rep appears stuck, disengaging, or giving up
    Signals: "I don't know", "I give up", "I'm lost", repeated short replies, explicit confusion
• stress_signal — true if the rep appears pressured, overloaded, or frantic (distinct from frustration)
    Signals: stacking multiple questions at once, time-pressure language ("quickly", "I need to"),
    fragmented bursts, juggling too many topics simultaneously, "how do I handle X AND Y AND Z?"
• engagement_level — quality of participation (binary)
    "passive" → minimal effort, very short responses, formulaic or one-word replies
    "engaged" → substantive response, uses domain knowledge, asks follow-ups, wants to practice more

Example:
  "affect": {"rep_confidence": "high", "frustration_signal": false, "stress_signal": false, "engagement_level": "engaged"}
