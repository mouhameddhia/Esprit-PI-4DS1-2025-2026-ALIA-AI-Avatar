━━━ AFFECT ━━━
affect is a nested object assessing the physician's communicative state. Keys:
• query_urgency — clinical urgency expressed in the query
    "routine"  → standard clinical question, no time pressure indicated
    "elevated" → language implies concern or importance ("important", "worried", "need to check")
    "urgent"   → explicit urgency ("immediately", "emergency", "urgent", "right now", "ASAP")
• frustration_signal — true if the physician appears frustrated or impatient
    Signals: terse frustrated phrasing, "I need this now", escalating or repeated questioning
• stress_signal — true if the physician appears overloaded or pressured by a complex situation
    Signals: stacking multiple clinical questions in one message, multiple concurrent drug concerns,
    "patient is in front of me right now", "I have 3 issues at once", fragmented multi-part queries
